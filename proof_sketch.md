# Proof sketch: boundedness of the (SSR)* turtle on every Penrose rhomb tiling

## Setting

* A Penrose rhomb tiling T (the local-isomorphism class of de Bruijn's pentagrid tilings with
  sum of offsets 0, equivalently the tilings by the two Penrose rhombs obeying the arrow matching
  rules, equivalently the substitution/inflation tilings; classical: de Bruijn 1981, Penrose,
  Baake–Grimm *Aperiodic Order* vol. 1 ch. 6–7).
* Turtle state (c, e): tile c and edge index e ∈ {0,1,2,3}; edges are listed in counter-clockwise
  vertex order, edge e joins vertex e and vertex e+1 (mod 4).
  S: cross edge e into the neighbouring rhomb c′; the shared edge has index e′ in c′; new state
     (c′, (e′+2) mod 4).  (This continues straight along the de Bruijn ribbon.)
  R: (c, e) → (c, (e+3) mod 4)  (turn the heading clockwise);  L: (c, e) → (c, (e+1) mod 4).
  A program P (word over S,R,L) is applied cyclically.  The step map is a bijection on states, so
  every orbit is either periodic or infinite-and-injective; an infinite injective orbit visits
  infinitely many distinct tiles, hence is unbounded.  So bounded ⇔ closed (periodic).
  The orbit of P from (c,e) depends only on the tiles visited and their edge adjacencies.

## Robinson-triangle substitution (exact)

Each thin rhomb is cut along its short diagonal into two acute golden triangles (36°,72°,72°),
each fat rhomb along its long diagonal into two obtuse golden triangles (108°,36°,36°).
Triangles carry an orientation label (the order of the two base vertices), which encodes the
Penrose arrow markings.  The substitution σ (inflate by φ then subdivide; the standard rule,
e.g. preshing.com "Penrose tiling explained"):
  acute (A,B,C):  P = φA + (B−A);  children acute(C′,P,B′), obtuse(P,C′,A′)  (X′ = φX)
  obtuse (A,B,C): Q = φB + (A−B), R = φB + (C−B); children obtuse(R,C′,A′), obtuse(Q,R,B′), acute(R,Q,A′)
All coordinates are kept as integer vectors in Z^5 (embedding K ↦ Σ K_j ζ^j, ζ = e^{2πi/5};
multiplication by φ = 1+ζ+ζ^4 and rotation by 36° are integer maps), so the geometry is exact.
Facts used: (F1) the marked triangle tilings form one LI class, closed under σ and σ^{-1}
(unique composition, Solomyak 1998); every such tiling is the union of its level-n supertiles
σ^n(u), u a triangle of the n-fold composed tiling.  (F2) linear repetitivity: every finite
patch occurs in every sufficiently large ball.  (F3) the unmarked rhomb tilings obtained by
merging triangle pairs across their bases are exactly the Penrose rhomb tilings (pentagrid
tilings).  The turtle only sees the unmarked rhomb tiling.
Empirical cross-check of the implementation: the set of first coronas (tile + all tiles touching
it) of the substitution patches coincides with that of the pentagrid tiling generated by the
paper's code; the (SR)* and (SSR)* class tables coincide on both.

## Step 1 — atlas of marked triangle coronas and closure

corona(t) := t together with all triangles sharing at least one vertex with t (as a marked patch).
A := the set of coronas occurring in σ^8(sun-seed), up to translation and rotation by multiples of
36° (not reflection): 30 classes.  (The 10-triangle wheel itself is not a legal patch, but σ(wheel)
is: it occurs inside σ^10(acute); hence σ^8(wheel) = σ^7(σ(wheel)) is a legal patch and every corona
in A is a corona of the LI class.  The same 30 classes are obtained from σ^10(acute) and σ^10(obtuse).)
Closure check (verified by computer): for every C ∈ A with centre t, and every child triangle
t′ ⊂ σ(t) of σ(C), the corona of t′ is complete inside σ(C) (angle sum 360° at its three
vertices) and its class is in A.
Lemma (closure ⇒ completeness). Geometric fact: if t′ ⊂ σ(u) then corona(t′) ⊂ σ(corona(u)),
because a triangle touching t′ lies in some σ(u″) with u″ touching u (supertiles are closed sets
tiling the plane; if u″ does not touch u then σ(u″) does not touch σ(u)).  Induction: all coronas
of triangles inside σ^n(t_C) are in A for every n and every C ∈ A.  Since σ^n(t_C) contains
arbitrarily large balls and (F2) every corona type occurs in every large ball, A contains every
corona of every tiling in the LI class.

## Step 2 — verification

For every C ∈ A: build the patch σ^7(C) (exact), merge its triangles into rhombs (dropping
unpaired boundary triangles), and from every rhomb containing at least one triangle of
σ^7(t_C) and every heading e ∈ {0,1,2,3}, run (SSR)* inside this finite patch.  An attempt to
cross an edge whose other side is not in the patch is recorded as an escape.  Result: 54,240
starts, all closed, 0 escapes, 0 non-convergent; periods ∈ {10,12,28,30,50,80,100}.
(Margin: the distance from the centre triangle to the complement of its corona is ≥ sin 36° ≈
0.588 for all C; at level 7 this is ≥ 17.07 > the maximal orbit diameter ≈ 8.5, which is why no
escape can happen in the interior of the supertile.)

## Step 3 — conclusion

Let T be any Penrose rhomb tiling, r_0 a rhomb, e a heading.  Choose a triangle t_0 of r_0.  By
(F1) t_0 ⊂ σ^7(u) for a unique level-7 supertriangle σ^7(u), u a triangle of the 7-fold composed
tiling T_7 (also in the LI class), and corona_{T_7}(u) ∈ A by Step 1.  σ^7(corona(u)) is a
patch of T which contains every triangle touching σ^7(u) (same geometric fact), in particular
both triangles of r_0, and r_0 is one of the start rhombs of Step 2 for the class of
corona(u) (up to a rotation by a multiple of 36°, which commutes with the turtle rules since it
preserves orientation; translations obviously do).  Since the orbit computed inside the patch
never leaves it, the orbit in T is the same closed orbit.  ∎

The same pipeline with n = 9 (φ^9 sin36° ≈ 44.7 > 2·18.8) proves boundedness of
(SSRSSSSR)* = (SSR)(SSSSR): 360,920 starts, 0 escapes, periods ∈ {4,5,8,42,45,64,65,75,111,125,225,253,309}.

## Review notes (2026-09-30)

Independent adversarial review (5 dimensions) found no gap in the argument.  Two code robustness issues
were fixed afterwards and all certificates were regenerated: (i) the final verdict of prove2.py now
requires atlas closure AND a non-empty start set AND all starts closed; (ii) subst_fast.py no longer
packs coordinates into 12-bit fields (overflow at |K_j| >= 2048, i.e. level n >= 11; the runs used
n <= 10 with max |K_j| = 1934) but matches rows by exact column-wise lexsort.
